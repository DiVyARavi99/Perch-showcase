Looking for Recommendations

I’ve already read Virginia Woolf's To the Lighthouse and Sarah Orne Jewett's The Country of the Pointed Firs. Seeking something translated or twentieth-century European that doesn't feel manufactured.

• 7 quiet suggestions
Clara Sol Lisbon, PT
18m ago

Immediately look for The Evening Star by Christian Signol or Island of the Doomed by Stig Dagerman. But if you want pure salt and solitude, read The Outermost House by Henry Beston. Every paragraph tastes like kelp and cold dawn.

Liam Byrne Galway, IE
2h ago

Look up That They May Face the Rising Sun by John McGahern. Not strictly a seaside storm novel, but it treats rainfall, lake wind, and neighbours borrowing small machinery with the absolute highest literary reverence. Zero murders, all grace.
