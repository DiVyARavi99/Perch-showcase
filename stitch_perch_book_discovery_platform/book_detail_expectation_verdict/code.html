Drive Your Plow Over the Bones of the Dead

by Olga Tokarczuk • Riverhead Books (Penguin Random House) • August 2019

In a remote Polish village, Janina Duszejko—an eccentric recluse who prefers the company of animals and studies astrology—finds herself thrust into a murder investigation when local hunters begin turning up dead under inexplicable circumstances. A subversive, darkly comedic murder mystery that doubles as an existential defense of animal rights and cosmos-bound justice.
