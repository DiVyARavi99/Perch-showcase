## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a sanctuary for thoughtful readers—a digital salon centered on shared literary taste, deliberate marginalia, and human-curated libraries. The brand voice is cultured, authentic, perceptive, and gently tactile, eschewing aggressive dopamine loops and stark utilitarian tech tropes in favor of an atmosphere that feels like an afternoon spent in an independent, sun-lit library.

The visual direction combines **Editorial Tactility** with **Warm Literary Contemporary**:
- Surface treatments invoke heavyweight archival paper, tactile deckle edges, woven bookcloth, and debossed book spines.
- Interface gestures incorporate physical reading references: bookmark tabs, ribbon page-markers, subtle ruled rules, and authentic margin notations.
- Graphic restraint prioritizes editorial white space, organic line-work, and refined serif title-sets, creating an environment that feels both timeless and modern.

## Colors
The palette balances grounded botanical and mineral tones against warm, natural paper grounds to create a colorful yet deeply mature atmosphere.

### Core Tones
- **Primary (`#1B3B2B`)**: Deep Forest Green. Conveys prestige, stillness, and historical library bindings. Used for primary interactive actions, high-emphasis status markers, and featured curation tags.
- **Secondary (`#B85D3B`)**: Rich Terracotta. Evokes fired clay, leather-bound corners, and warm ink. Reserved for secondary highlights, community badges, and energetic reading milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#D4973B`)**: Ochre Amber. A radiant, intellectual tone for quotes, annotations, star ratings, and subtle warm notifications.
- **Neutral Primary (`#1E1B18`)**: Deep Slate Espresso. Replaces harsh blacks for all typography and structural iconography, ensuring high legibility with an organic ink quality.

### Accent Accompanying Shades
- **Muted Periwinkle (`#6C7D93`)**: Represents quiet reflection and analytical critique; ideal for genre chips and shelf statuses (e.g., "Currently Reading").
- **Dusty Rose (`#C48B82`)**: Subtle sentiment; used for personal bookmarks, favorited passages, and author focus tags.

### Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF7F2`)**: Crisp archival parchment; soft on the eyes during prolonged reading sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F4EFE6`)**: Pressed linen paper for cards, floating panels, and shelf compartments.
- **Border Rim (`#E3DAC9`)**: Tonal bone-line border for structural containment without artificial drop-shadow noise.

## Typography
The typographic balance relies on an intentional counterpoint between historic literary gravitas and functional modern readability.

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`)**: Characterized by warm transitions, elegant brackets, and expressive italic forms. Italicized cuts are recommended for book titles, reviewer asides, and pulleted blockquotes.
- **Interface & Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, highly legible, modern geometry with friendly apertures that avoids cold tech sterility while retaining clarity in dense book reviews and discussion threads.
- **Editorial Quotations (`quote-editorial`)**: A dedicated role tailored specifically for excerpts, margin notations, and direct literary citations, styled with an organic indent or border-left trace.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a structured, fluid 12-column grid on desktop screens, consolidating to an 8-column layout on tablet and a single/two-column dynamic flow on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px)**: 12 columns, max content container width of `1280px`, with generous outer canvas margins (`3rem`) to frame the page like open leaves of a volume.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, gutters set to `1.25rem`, margins scaled down to `2rem`.
- **Mobile (under 768px)**: Fluid reading stack with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins ensuring books and reviews occupy high-touch, thumb-accessible central visual lanes.

Spacing values prioritize vertical rhythm inspired by standard book point grids. Layouts must balance white space around critical interactions, preventing the chaotic clutter standard in generic social feeds.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is expressed through physical tactile layering rather than synthesized drop shadows. The system uses a **Tonal Paper Stacking** model:

1. **Ground Layer (`#FAF7F2`)**: The foundational parchment substrate.
2. **Elevated Surfaces (`#F4EFE6`)**: Shelves, review containers, and profile headers. Defined by a continuous 1px hairline border (`#E3DAC9`) rather than heavy shadows.
3. **Floating Overlays & Modals (`#FFFFFF`)**: Popovers, book quick-actions, and contextual margin comments. Lifted with an ultra-diffused, warm-tinted ambient shadow (`box-shadow: 0 8px 30px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.04)`).
4. **Physical Embellishments**:
   - **Debossed Depths**: Form inputs and shelf recesses utilize a subtle inner line (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(30, 27, 24, 0.05)`).
   - **Page Edges**: Multi-volume book views use staggered horizontal 1px line offsets to subtly signify stacked sheets.

## Shapes
The shape system employs modest, soft geometry (`roundedness: 1` / 4px base radius) to evoke trimmed book blocks, archival library index cards, and printed bookplates.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem`)**: Standard interactive elements, badges, input fields, and inline action buttons.
- **Medium Radius (`0.5rem`)**: Book jacket containers, review cards, contextual dialogue boxes.
- **Large Radius (`0.75rem`)**: Modal windows, custom bookshelf dividers, reading group profile containers.
- **Exceptions**: Author/reader avatars are circular, while bookmark ribbons retain angled or notched bottom edges.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons**: Background in Deep Forest Green (`#1B3B2B`), text in Crisp Parchment (`#FAF7F2`), subtle top-edge bevel via 1px inner highlight, hover state transitioning to a deep rich patina (`#142D21`).
- **Secondary Buttons**: Unfilled with a 1px border in `#1B3B2B`, typography in `#1B3B2B`, and interactive hover fills utilizing Terracotta tint at 8% opacity.
- **Bookmark Actions**: Micro-actions styled as top-anchored ribbon tags that dynamically "tuck" into card seams when active.

### Chips & Social Curation Tags
- Flat parchment backings (`#F4EFE6`) with soft borders (`#E3DAC9`).
- Categorical chips utilize intentional muted color codes: Dusty Rose for thematic moods, Muted Periwinkle for standard genres, and Ochre Amber for reading progress (e.g., "75% Read").

### Book Cards & Review Modules
- Designed with index-card proportions. Each card incorporates an optional left-side color swatch indicating reader recommendation strength or trusted curator tier.
- Metadata is arranged with clean hierarchy: author name in uppercase letter-spaced sans-serif, title set in Newsreader italic, and the reviewer's personal margin note set in an offset cream blockquote.

### Margin Note & Trust Badges
- **Margin Notes**: Inline comment threads designed as editorial marginalia, positioned alongside review bodies with a dashed ochre anchor rule.
- **Trust Badges**: Compact heraldic seals featuring botanical motifs and delicate typography, validating readers who consistently offer thoughtful critique.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Inputs
- Text fields use parchment base (`#FAF7F2`), an inset border in `#E3DAC9`, and focus rings in Forest Green (`#1B3B2B`) with a soft 2px ambient tint spread.
- Checkboxes and radios feature square-turned-soft profiles with solid botanical ink check-marks upon selection.
