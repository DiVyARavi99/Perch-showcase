## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a calm, contemplative sanctuary for long-form readers, literary critics, and essayists. It rejects the hyper-stimulating, dopamine-driven aesthetics of conventional social platforms in favor of editorial restraint, intellectual breathing room, and deliberate pacing.

The visual style blends modern editorial minimalism with subtle organic warmth:
- **Atmospheric Calm:** Generous negative space and unhurried vertical rhythms mirror the visual cadence of premium print quarterlies.
- **Understated Precision:** Rather than relying on heavy dividers or harsh outlines, structural hierarchy is established through tone-on-tone shifts, crisp typography, and architectural alignment.
- **Tactile Softness:** Warm oat and parchment backdrops temper digital glare, creating an environment that feels grounded, literary, and easy on the eyes during prolonged reading sessions.

## Colors

The color palette reflects natural bookbinding materials: deep forest moss, dried sage leaf, terracotta book cloth, and archival paper stock.

### Color Roles & Surface Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#163323` - Deep Botanical Pine):** Reserved for primary navigation states, high-priority interactive calls to action, active tab indicators, and primary section headers. Conveys authority, grounding, and scholarly focus.
- **Secondary (`#4A7C59` - Soft Sage):** Used for inline tags, progress indicators (reading completion bars), subtle accent badges, and secondary interactive states.
- **Tertiary (`#C96E4B` - Warm Clay):** Serves as an expressive editorial accent for curation markers, bookmarks, quote highlights, and deliberate focal calls (e.g., "Add Annotation", "Staff Pick").
- **Neutral Primary (`#2D312E` - Muted Slate/Ink):** The primary text color. Softened from pure black to mimic archival printing ink on natural paper.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#5C635E`):** Used for metadata, author bylines, reading time estimates, and secondary labels.
- **Neutral Muted (`#8C938E`):** Employed for timestamps, inactive icons, placeholder hints, and subtle count markers.

### Surface Tones
- **Base Canvas (`#FBF9F5` - Pale Parchment):** The primary background tone for full-bleed viewport layouts.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#F4EFEA` - Warm Oat):** Used for side navigation bars, secondary utility rails, and backdrop fills.
- **Border & Subtle Fill (`#EAE3DC` - Linen Grey):** Applied to whisper-thin horizontal separation lines, hairline boundaries, and active row fills.
- **Card Surface (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp White):** Used exclusively for interactive reader cards, essay teasers, and content modal windows to provide clean visual lift against the parchment canvas.

## Typography

The typographic strategy moves away from decorative serifs in favor of an exceptionally crisp, geometric sans-serif system engineered for sustained reading on digital displays.

- **Headline Hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Selected for its balanced geometry, subtle warmth, and clear apertures. Headings must always maintain tight negative letter spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor long titles without feeling heavy or industrial.
- **Reading Body (`Inter`):** Optimized for continuous readability. Body line heights are deliberately tall (`1.65` to `1.875` ratio) to provide expansive horizontal scanning tracks, ensuring essays and user reviews remain uncluttered.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (`Inter`):** Small meta details, tags, and categorical indicators use increased tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.04em`) to maintain sharp legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a spacious, asymmetric 3-column desktop layout that grants primacy to reading matter.

### Desktop Architecture (1280px and wider)
- **Left Navigation Rail (Fixed / 260px):** Uncluttered persistent navigation containing identity, core feeds, reading lists, and personal collections.
- **Center Reading Stream (Fluid / Max 720px):** Dedicated single-column or card-based feed designed with a comfortable max-width to protect line-length readability (55–75 characters per line).
- **Right Contextual Rail (Sticky / 320px):** Reading goals, trending marginalia, curated reading clubs, and contextual quotes.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 3-column layout. Margin: `2.5rem`, Gutter: `2rem`.
- **Tablet / Small Laptop (768px – 1279px):** 2-column layout. The right contextual rail collapses into an expandable drawer or bottom-docked utility sheet. Margin: `2rem`, Gutter: `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Persistent navigation shifts to a minimal top bar and bottom quick-access rail. Margin: `1.25rem`, Gutter: `1rem`.

### Spacing Principles
- Never pack interactive elements together. Use `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) between related content modules and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) between distinct thematic sections.
- Reader card internal padding relies on `space-lg` across desktop viewports to give text breathing room against white edges.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed almost entirely through tonal layering, subtle opacity, and ambient, diffused warm shadows. Harsh drop shadows and heavy dark borders are strictly prohibited.

- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBF9F5`. Completely flat, non-elevated ground plane.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces positioned over `#FBF9F5` or `#F4EFEA`. Depth is achieved with an ultra-soft, warm-tinted ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(45, 49, 46, 0.02)`. Border is set to an imperceptible `1px solid rgba(234, 227, 220, 0.6)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active States):** For interactive reader cards on hover: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(22, 51, 35, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(22, 51, 35, 0.03)`, with a subtle `translateY(-1px)` shift.
- **Level 3 (Overlays & Dialogs):** Modals, reader settings, and contextual annotation palettes: `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(22, 51, 35, 0.12)`. Supported by a frosted backdrop overlay (`background: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary balances architectural clarity with welcoming organic touches.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to standard interactive elements including text input boxes, dropdown selectors, inline code/excerpt containers, and buttons.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`rounded-lg` - 1rem / 16px):** Standard curvature for main reader cards, list panels, modal sheets, and side-panel utility widgets.
- **Pill Geometry (Full Rounded):** Strictly applied to status tags, category chips, reading progress capsules, and avatar frames.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Pine (`#163323`) background, `#FFFFFF` text. Flat finish, 0.5rem radius, padded with `0.625rem 1.25rem`. Hover state introduces a gentle tint shift to `#204530`.
- **Secondary / Soft:** Faint Sage tint (`rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.12)`) with `#163323` text. No border. On hover, background deepens to `rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.18)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No fill or border. Text is `#5C635E` transitioning to `#163323` on hover with a slight background tint (`#F4EFEA`).
- **Accent (Clay):** Solid `#C96E4B` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for highlight/bookmark creation and critical engagement actions.

### Reader Cards & Essay Snippets
- Base fill: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) on parchment background.
- Radius: `1rem`. Internal padding: `1.5rem` desktop, `1.125rem` mobile.
- Hairline outline: `1px solid rgba(234, 227, 220, 0.8)`.
- Header section features author meta in `Neutral Secondary` alongside a `Label-sm` reading time pill.
- Title in `Headline-sm` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), followed by body excerpt in `Body-md` (`Inter`) with generous line-height (`1.5rem`).

### Chips & Filters
- **Default State:** Transparent fill with `1px solid #EAE3DC` border, `#5C635E` text, `0.75rem` vertical and `1rem` horizontal padding, pill-shaped.
- **Selected State:** `#163323` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, border remains `#163323`.
- **Accent Chip:** Warm Oat fill (`#F4EFEA`) with Warm Clay text (`#C96E4B`) for curated reading categories and thematic tags.

### Input Fields & Search
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFEA`.
- Border: `1px solid #EAE3DC`. Active/Focus state utilizes a clean ring: `border-color: #4A7C59` and `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(74, 124, 89, 0.15)`.
- Typography: `Body-md` text with `#8C938E` placeholder color.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Base: Unchecked box uses a crisp `1.5px` border in `#8C938E` against `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Solid `#163323` with white checkmark icon or center dot. Radius: `0.25rem` for checkboxes, full circle for radios.

### Specialized Literary Components
- **Marginalia / Inline Annotation Drawer:** Floating side-card pinned adjacent to essay text. Background `#FFFFFF`, left accent boundary using a 3px vertical bar in Warm Clay (`#C96E4B`).
- **Reading Progress Bar:** Sleek 2px progress bar anchored at the top of the browser viewport or card top, filled with Soft Sage (`#4A7C59`).
- **Quote Callout:** Indented block on `#F4EFEA` background, `0.75rem` radius, italicized Inter body, bordered exclusively on the left edge with a 3px stroke of `#163323`.
