The Northern Taiga

By Elena Rostova • Advised by Prof. Clara Vane

grade
Score 96.4/100

Set across the Siberian permafrost research outpost fifty years after the Great Thaw, The Northern Taiga tracks three generations of glaciologists who uncover a dormant vegetative intelligence capable of rewriting ecological memory. Rostova deftly combines acute botanical lyricism with harrowing emotional precision.

format_quote

"The ice did not melt into water; it unlocked into speech. Every trapped bubble of air from eight thousand winters exhaled at once against the glass of the observation dome, smelling faintly of prehistoric moss."
